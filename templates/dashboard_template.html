CHAPTER 04

The same sale can tell three stories.

First touch credits the campaign that introduced a lead. Last touch credits the final recorded channel. Linear spreads credit across unique channels in that lead's path. The methods are useful views of the record, not proof of what caused a purchase.

Revenue credited by channel

The filters apply to the original lead cohort. A change in attribution credit does not change total booked revenue; it changes where that revenue is recorded.

The touchpoint paths are synthetic examples. Use this comparison to identify campaigns whose credit changes by method, then investigate with holdout tests or an incrementality design.

CHAPTER 05

Try a budget move on paper first.

Pick a source channel, a destination channel and a transfer size. The sandbox applies each channel's historical average lead cost, win rate and booked revenue per lead to the proposed shift.

10%
This is arithmetic, not a forecast. It assumes historical rates stay constant and ignores saturation, audience overlap, auction changes and sales capacity. Use it to form a testable question, not to reallocate the whole budget.
Project note: The files in this repo use synthetic data created for demonstration.
